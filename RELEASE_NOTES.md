**Download `Roicon.exe`** and run it. No install or setup needed.

### What's new in 1.1.0
- **Sign in with Roblox is built in.** Press Connect, pick your account or group on Roblox's page, press Select. Nothing to paste or configure.
- **Your Roblox profile in the corner.** Your avatar, display name and @username sit bottom-left; click it to view your Roblox profile, open settings or disconnect.
- **16+ and private games work.** Roicon reads games Roblox hides publicly through your sign-in, and fills in the owner before uploading.
- **Cleaner Connect screen** that always opens at the top.
- Open Cloud API key sign-in is still available under Settings → Advanced.

Windows 10/11. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the app isn't code-signed yet: choose "More info" → "Run anyway".
