**Download `Roicon.exe`** and run it. It installs itself, adds a Desktop and Start menu shortcut, and opens. Already on 1.2.0? It updates by itself.

### What's new in 1.3.0
- **New logo.** A glowing hourglass with amber-to-pink sand.
- **A smoother, livelier UI.**
  - Pages glide in when you switch experiences or tabs.
  - The selected experience and the active tab slide to their new spot.
  - Buttons, switches and cards ease in and out on hover.
- **Animated "Update in" card.** It has drifting lights, a live pulse and a progress bar counting down to the update.
- Steps and timeline icons fade in one after another. Timeline icons lift when you hover them.
- Pop-ups fade in, and notifications slide in and out.
- New violet-to-magenta accents to match the logo.

Windows 10/11. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the app isn't code-signed yet: choose "More info" → "Run anyway".
