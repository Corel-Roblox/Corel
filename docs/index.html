Four steps

Set it up once. Corel does the rest.

01

Connect Roblox

Sign in with Roblox and pick the account or group that owns your game.

02

Plan the update

Choose your current icon, your new icon and when the update drops.

03

04

It goes live

The icon switches on every place at each step, then the new one goes up.

Features

Made for update day.

Everything runs on your PC and talks only to Roblox.

Hourglass countdowns

Ready-made 12, 6, 3 and 1 hour overlays, or drop in your own PNGs for any timer.

Approved before it's needed

Icons upload early and Corel tracks moderation, so nothing goes up unapproved.

Every place at once

Lobbies, arenas and sub-places all switch together. Turn places on or off per game.

Watch from your phone

Scan a QR code to follow the countdown live over your home Wi-Fi.

Always up to date

Installs in one click with a desktop shortcut, and updates itself in the background.

Private by design

No servers, no tracking. Sign-in is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC.

Permissions

What Corel asks Roblox for.

Corel uses Roblox's official sign-in (OAuth 2.0). You choose which accounts it can use, and you can disconnect any time.
