Publish

Your icon counts down. On autopilot.

Pick your new icon and an update time. Corel builds countdown icons, uploads them early so Roblox approves them in time, and switches your icon at every step, on every place.

  • Custom steps. 12h, 6h, 3h, 1h, or your own timings and overlay art.
  • Announcements with a live preview, copied straight to Discord.
  • Honest status. A step shows live only after Roblox accepted it.
  • Team-wide. Everyone sees what’s scheduled and when.
