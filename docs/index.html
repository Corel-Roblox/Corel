Your icon counts down.

Roicon puts a countdown on your Roblox game icon before an update, then switches to the new icon right on time. On autopilot.

Download for Windows View source
Installs in one click, adds a desktop shortcut and updates itself. Free and open source. Not affiliated with Roblox Corporation.
1

Connect Roblox

Sign in with Roblox and pick the account or group that owns your game.

2

Plan the update

Choose your current icon, your new icon and when the update drops.

3

Approved early

Every countdown icon is uploaded ahead of time so it passes moderation before it's needed.

4

It goes live

Roicon switches the icon on each of your places at every step, then puts up the new one.

See it in action

Sign in with Roblox, add a game, pick the new icon and the time. Roicon uploads the countdown icons early so they pass moderation.

Make your own timers

Use the built-in hourglass countdowns or drop in your own transparent 512×512 overlays. They're placed on top of your current icon at each step.

What Roicon asks Roblox for

Roicon uses Roblox's official sign-in (OAuth 2.0). You choose which accounts it can use, and you can disconnect at any time.

PermissionWhyRisk level
Read profileShow your username and avatar in the appLow
Read assetsCheck that your countdown icons passed moderationLow
Write assetsUpload the countdown icons and set them as your place iconsMedium

Your phone takes a look

Scan a QR code to follow the countdown live on your phone over your home Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through our servers. Roicon doesn't have any.
