Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Corel is a Windows desktop app for Roblox development teams: projects, icon publishing, analytics, bug tracking, a shared script library and team collaboration. This policy explains what information Corel uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

Information Corel uses

  • Your workspace account: email address, password (stored only as a hash by the authentication service), display name, optional username, Roblox username, bio and avatar picture.
  • What you and your team create: teams, members and roles, projects, tasks, bug reports and attachments, comments, scripts and their versions, publishing schedules, imported analytics, activity history and notifications.
  • Presence: whether you are online and which page or script you have open, shown to members of your team while the app is running, plus a "last active" time.
  • Your Roblox account basics (user ID, username, display name and avatar), the accounts and groups you granted, and Roblox sign-in tokens, used to publish icons for you.
  • Public experience statistics (visits, players online, favorites, votes) that Roblox shows publicly for the experiences in your projects.

Where it is stored

  • Workspace data is stored in the Corel workspace server (a Supabase project) configured in your copy of the app, so it can be shared with your team and synced between devices. If you or your organisation connected your own server, that organisation operates it.
  • On your computer: publishing schedules and icon images in %APPDATA%\Corel, and your workspace sign-in, encrypted with your Windows account.
  • Roblox sign-in tokens and any API key you enter are stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC and are never sent to the workspace server.
  • Corel does not collect analytics or telemetry about how you use the app.

Who it is shared with

  • Members of your teams. Content you create in a team is visible to its members according to their roles and each project's visibility settings. Your profile (name, avatar, Roblox username, online status) is visible to people who share a team with you.
  • The workspace server provider (Supabase), which hosts the database, authentication, file storage and real-time sync on behalf of whoever operates the server.
  • Roblox. The app sends requests to Roblox's services to sign you in, read experience information and statistics, upload icon images, check moderation and set place icons. Uploaded images become assets on your Roblox account or group.
  • Your phone, if you use the phone relay, over your local network with a private link you can replace at any time.
  • Corel never sells or rents your information.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and notification preferences in Settings at any time.
  • Export a team's data as JSON from Settings → Data management.
  • Leave a team, or delete your account from Settings → Account security. Deleting your account removes your profile and any team you own alone; comments and activity you left in other teams remain, shown as from a former member.
  • Disconnect Roblox in Settings → Integrations to revoke and remove Corel's Roblox tokens. You can also remove Corel from the authorized apps in your Roblox account settings.

Children

Roblox's sign-in for third-party apps is available to users 13 and older. Corel is meant for creators who manage their own Roblox experiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
